# VOXA Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Engineering foundation
- Monorepo conventions, CI, environment management, code quality, observability.
- Identity and role model; API contracts; database migrations.
- Threat model, privacy map, product analytics plan.

## Phase 1 — Podcast core
- Registration, creator profiles, show and episode publishing.
- Upload pipeline, audio/video playback, search and discovery.
- Follow, save, resume, transcripts, chapters, comments.
- Creator studio and basic analytics.

## Phase 2 — VOXA LIVE
- LiveKit integration, room lifecycle, host/co-host roles.
- Short-lived room tokens and stage admission requests.
- Live chat, moderation, reactions, polls, Q&A.
- Recording, processing, replay, notifications.

## Phase 3 — Community
- Creator communities, posts, member roles, announcements.
- Membership entitlements, private events, discussion tools.

## Phase 4 — VOXA COMMERCE
- Payment provider abstraction and market capability matrix.
- Subscriptions, tips, virtual gifts, tickets, advertising/sponsorship workflows.
- Double-entry ledger, creator earnings, payout verification, refunds and reconciliation.

## Phase 5 — VOXA Moments and growth
- Creator-approved clips, rights controls, sharing, referrals.
- Recommendation systems, localization, creator tools, experimentation.

## Launch gates
Do not launch publicly until critical security testing, moderation staffing, support coverage, payment reconciliation, privacy/legal review, load tests, backup/restore tests, and incident response drills pass. Roll out by controlled cohorts and markets even if the product is designed for global availability from day one.
